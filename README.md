# 🛒 Super Market Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Super Market Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales data to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate business performance across different branches and product categories.

Using **Microsoft Excel**, the project involves data cleaning, data transformation, pivot table analysis, and interactive dashboard creation to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides a clear visualization of key performance indicators (KPIs), sales distribution, customer preferences, and branch-wise performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total sales and revenue performance.
* Compare sales across different branches.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Analyze payment methods and customer types.
* Evaluate customer ratings and satisfaction.
* Create an interactive dashboard for business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel**
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts

* Data Cleaning

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

* **Sales Analysis:** Monthly and overall sales performance.
* **Branch Analysis:** Compare sales across Brooklyn and Queens.
* **Product Analysis:** Examine sales by product line.
* **Customer Analysis:** Compare Member and Normal customers.
* **Payment Analysis:** Understand payment method preferences.
* **Customer Satisfaction:** Analyze customer ratings.

## 💡 Key Insights

* Identify branches generating the highest revenue.
* Discover product categories contributing significantly to sales.
* Understand differences in spending between customer types.
* Identify popular payment methods.
* Track monthly sales trends and customer ratings.

## 📈 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates practical data analytics skills, including data preparation, exploratory data analysis, KPI tracking, and dashboard development. It showcases how Excel can transform raw supermarket transaction data into actionable business insights.
